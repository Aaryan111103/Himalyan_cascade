Sidebar Inputs (st.sidebar.slider): Creates
dynamic controls on the left side. Adjusting the
rainfall slider recalculated all risks in real time.
Connecting Stage 2 (cascade_engine.py):
Imports your functions at the top and runs
calculations instantly as inputs change.
Map Setup (folium): Draws an interactive map. If
the risk passes 65%, a black warning icon
appears over the river along with a blue line
marking the flood wave trajectory.
Alert Cards (st.metric & st.error): Shows clear
numerical risk scores and flash flood wave

arrival time countdowns for disaster authorities
